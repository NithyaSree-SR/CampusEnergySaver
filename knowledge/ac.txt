AIR CONDITIONER ENERGY SAVING GUIDELINES

Air conditioners can consume significant electricity, especially when used for long periods.

Energy-saving practices for air conditioners:
- Avoid unnecessarily low temperature settings.
- Use a reasonable temperature setting while maintaining occupant comfort.
- Turn off air conditioners when a room is unoccupied for an extended period.
- Avoid running air conditioners in empty classrooms or laboratories.
- Keep doors and windows closed while air conditioning is operating.
- Regular maintenance and clean filters can help maintain efficient operation.
- Use air conditioning according to actual occupancy and room requirements.
- Energy savings depend on the AC model, capacity, temperature, operating duration and room conditions.

Safety and comfort:
- Energy-saving recommendations should not compromise health, safety or required indoor comfort.
- The system should recommend actions rather than automatically changing AC settings.
