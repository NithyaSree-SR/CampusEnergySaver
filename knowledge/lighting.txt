LIGHTING ENERGY SAVING GUIDELINES

Lighting is an important part of energy consumption in classrooms and laboratories.

Energy-saving practices for lighting:
- Switch off lights when a room is unoccupied.
- Switch off unnecessary lights when sufficient natural light is available.
- Avoid keeping all lights switched on when only part of the room is occupied.
- Use energy-efficient lighting where possible.
- Match the number of lights in use with the actual requirement of the room.
- Regularly check lighting systems for unnecessary operation.
- Lighting recommendations should consider visibility, safety and the activities being performed.

Campus lighting:
- Empty classrooms and laboratories should not have unnecessary lights running for long periods.
- When occupancy is low, unnecessary lighting can be switched off while maintaining adequate lighting for the people using the room.
- Energy-saving estimates are approximate and depend on the type and power rating of the lighting equipment.
