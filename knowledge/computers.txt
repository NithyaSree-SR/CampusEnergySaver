COMPUTER AND MONITOR ENERGY SAVING GUIDELINES

Computers and monitors can consume unnecessary electricity when they remain powered on without being used.

Energy-saving practices for computers:
- Switch off computers when they are not required for an extended period.
- Avoid leaving computers running when a laboratory or classroom is unoccupied.
- Switch off or put monitors into an appropriate low-power state when not needed.
- Shut down computers after the end of laboratory sessions when they will not be used again.
- Avoid keeping unnecessary computers powered on when only a small number are required.
- Use the number of computers appropriate for the actual number of users.
- Energy-saving decisions should consider the required tasks and working conditions.

Campus computer laboratory:
- If many computers are running while only a few students are present, the system can identify this as potential energy wastage.
- Unused computers can be recommended for shutdown after checking that they are not required for ongoing work.
- The system should recommend actions rather than automatically shutting down computers.
- Energy-saving estimates are approximate and depend on computer specifications, power consumption and duration of use.
