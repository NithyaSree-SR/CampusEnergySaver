FAN ENERGY SAVING GUIDELINES

Fans consume electricity while operating and should be used according to actual room requirements.

Energy-saving practices for fans:
- Switch off fans when a room is unoccupied.
- Avoid running unnecessary fans when only part of a room is occupied.
- Use the number of fans required for the actual occupancy and activity.
- Fans should be used together with air conditioning only when appropriate for comfort and energy efficiency.
- Regular maintenance can help fans operate efficiently.

Campus fan usage:
- In classrooms and laboratories, unnecessary fans should be switched off when the room is empty.
- When occupancy is low, unnecessary fans can be switched off while maintaining adequate comfort for occupants.
- The system should recommend actions rather than automatically controlling fans.


PROJECTOR ENERGY SAVING GUIDELINES

Projectors consume electricity while operating and may waste energy when left running unnecessarily.

Energy-saving practices for projectors:
- Switch off the projector when it is not required.
- Avoid leaving the projector running during long breaks.
- Switch off the projector after the end of a class or presentation.
- Use the projector only when required for teaching or presentations.
- Allow the projector to cool down according to the manufacturer's requirements before disconnecting power.

Campus projector usage:
- Projectors in classrooms should not remain powered on when no presentation or teaching activity is taking place.
- The system can identify a projector running without an active class or presentation as potential energy wastage.
- The system should recommend switching off the projector rather than automatically controlling it.
