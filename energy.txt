ENERGY SAVING GUIDELINES

Energy conservation means reducing unnecessary energy consumption while maintaining required comfort, safety and productivity.

General energy saving practices:
- Switch off lights when a room is unoccupied.
- Switch off computers and monitors when they are not required.
- Avoid running electrical equipment unnecessarily.
- Use energy-efficient equipment where possible.
- Match energy usage with actual room occupancy and requirements.
- Regularly monitor energy consumption to identify waste.
- Avoid keeping multiple appliances running when they are not needed.
- Energy-saving decisions should not compromise safety or necessary working conditions.

Campus energy management:
- Classrooms and laboratories should avoid unnecessary operation of lights, computers and air conditioners.
- Equipment should be switched off when a room is empty for an extended period.
- Energy-saving recommendations should consider occupancy, duration of use and required comfort.
- Energy consumption estimates are approximate and may vary depending on equipment specifications, electricity tariffs and actual usage.
